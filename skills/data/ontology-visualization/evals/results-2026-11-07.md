criteria. Outputs stayed in temporary workspaces. No publication or store

## Focused follow-up

An audit checked the reports against their evidence files, archive contents and
recorded hashes. The reported test-group totals and artifact identities matched.
It found one overstatement: the update report said its validator checked the
exact release delta, but the validator accepted invented statement-list entries.
The actual delivered delta was correct.

The follow-up reproduced three defects in isolated copies: delayed clipboard
completion affected a later term, an evaluator overwrote an existing audit
report, and delta validation checked too little. Corrected copies passed tests
for delayed success and rejection, leaving the term view, report containment
and no-clobber behavior, and exact deltas derived independently from raw RDF.
The delta suite recorded twelve outcomes, including nine expected rejections.
Invented and missing additions/removals failed even with recomputed hashes.
Literal identity and blank-node scope remained intact.

Complete-output checks also rejected altered script and notice bytes with
updated self-recorded hashes. Permission checks covered full POSIX modes. The
corrected viewer ZIP passed the focused browser checks after extraction. Earlier
outputs were preserved. These observed defects informed the playbook's focused
checks; the skill does not copy the trial application or its test harness.

## Coverage limits

The trials used Chrome and small fixtures. They do not establish large-graph
usability, Firefox or WebKit behavior, or screen-reader coverage. Zoom checks
used CSS zoom, not native browser zoom. The neutral viewer has no pagination,
so page restoration and focus after paging remain unexercised in these trials.
Those cases were exercised in the originating project's viewer tests.

Repository and publication workflows were outside the temporary evaluation
scope. Their checks and reviews belong to the skill's pull request and each
consuming project. User review remains a condition when requested.
